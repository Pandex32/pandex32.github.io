How It Works Under the Hood

Reviewing legal contracts manually is slow and easy to get wrong. I led the architecture and backend team to build a modular WordPress plugin ([legal_ai_analyzer]) that turns raw legal documents into structured, plain-English insights.

Pipeline: PDF Upload or Pasted Text → PHP Text Extractor & Sanitizer → AI Engine Prompt Layer → Risk Report, Clause Audit, or E-Signed PDF

What I Built & Led

  • Multi-Mode Analysis: Built workflows for full contract scanning, HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW risk scoring, missing clause detection, key date extraction, and plain-English summaries.
  • Contract Drafting & E-Signatures: Added a contract generator tailored to English law paired with an HTML5 signature pad that embeds both parties’ signatures directly into a downloadable PDF.
  • Server-Side PDF Handling: Implemented PHP PDF extraction (up to 10MB) and formatted PDF report generation.
  • Security & Team Coordination: Enforced AJAX nonce checks, strict MIME-type validation, and clean Git workflows across the backend team.
