How It Works Under the Hood

The modular [legal_ai_analyzer] plugin turns legal documents into structured, plain-English insights through a focused analysis pipeline.

Pipeline: PDF upload or pasted text → PHP text extraction and sanitization → AI Engine prompt layer → risk report, clause audit, or e-signed PDF

What I Built & Led

  • Multi-mode legal analysis: Full contract scanning, HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW risk scoring, missing-clause detection, key-date extraction, and plain-English summaries.
  • English-law drafting and dual e-signatures: A contract generator tailored to English law with an HTML5 signature pad that embeds both parties’ signatures into a downloadable PDF.
  • Server-side PDF extraction and generation: PHP extraction for uploads up to 10MB and formatted PDF report generation.
  • AJAX and file security: Nonce checks and strict MIME-type validation protect requests and uploaded documents.
