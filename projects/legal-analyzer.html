How It Works Under the Hood

Reviewing legal contracts manually is slow and easy to get wrong. I led the architecture and backend team to build a modular WordPress plugin ([legal_ai_analyzer]) that turns raw legal documents into structured, plain-English insights.

Pipeline: PDF Upload or Pasted Text → PHP Text Extractor & Sanitizer → AI Engine Prompt Layer → Risk Report, Clause Audit, or E-Signed PDF

What I Built & Led

  • Multi-Mode Analysis: Full contract scanning, HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW risk scoring, missing clause detection, key date extraction, and plain-English summaries.
  • Contract Drafting & E-Signatures: English-law contract generation paired with an HTML5 signature pad for both parties.
  • Server-Side PDF Handling: PDF extraction and formatted report generation.
  • Security & Team Coordination: Strict MIME validation, secure input handling, and clean Git workflows.
